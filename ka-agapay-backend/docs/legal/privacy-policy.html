Ka-Agapay Privacy Notice

Effective [date of approval] · Version 1.0 · Applies to the Ka-Agapay mobile app and the RHU web system

Ka-Agapay is the health service system of the Rural Health Units of the Municipality of Malasiqui, Pangasinan. This notice explains what personal information it collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the rights you have over it under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. What we collect

InformationWhy
Account details: first and last name, mobile number, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), barangay To create your account, confirm who you are, and connect you to your RHU.
Profile: birth date, sex, civil status, address Required before booking a consultation, so the RHU can identify you and keep an accurate record.
Optional: guardian or emergency contact, PhilHealth number Only if you provide them. PhilHealth numbers are shown masked in the app.
Photo of a government ID To verify your identity when you register. Text is read from the image automatically (see section 3).
Health information: your reason for consultation, symptoms, allergies, maintenance medications, consultation notes written by RHU staff, diagnoses, prescriptions, laboratory requests and results, and follow-up schedules To provide care, keep your medical record, and arrange follow-ups. This is sensitive personal information under RA 10173.
Messages, photos and voice notes you send to the Ka-Agapay assistant To answer your question. See section 3 — these are processed by an AI service.
Video and audio during a telemedicine consultation To hold the consultation. Calls are not recorded.
Device notification token To send you reminders and updates about your appointments.
Basic activity records (for example, when you booked or joined a session) For security, troubleshooting, and the RHU's service records.

The app asks for camera permission (to photograph your ID or attach a photo) and microphone permission (for voice notes and video consultations). It does not access your location, contacts, or phone identifiers, and it does not use an advertising ID or show advertising.

You may ask for your account to be closed and your personal information deleted by contacting the DPO (section 1) [and, once added, from Profile → Delete account in the app]. We will confirm your identity first. Health records that the law requires the RHU to keep will be retained for the required period and then deleted; everything else will be deleted or anonymised within [period, e.g. 30 days].

6. Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is processed (this notice);
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • object to processing, or have it suspended, withdrawn, blocked or erased where the law allows;
  • obtain a copy of your data in a commonly used electronic format;
  • be compensated for damages from inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained or unauthorised use of personal data; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

To use any of these, contact the DPO.

7. How we protect it

All connections use HTTPS encryption. Staff see only what their role allows, and staff in one RHU cannot see another RHU's records unless their role covers both. Sign-in sessions expire. Data is backed up daily and restores are tested regularly.

8. Children

A parent or guardian should create and manage the account of a child under 18, and gives consent on the child's behalf. [Confirm the LGU's rule for minors.]

9. Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this notice and its effective date, and tell you in the app before the change applies.
